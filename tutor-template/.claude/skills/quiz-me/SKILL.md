# Quiz

## Steps

1. Scope it. Only material actually taught — check the progress file, not the course
   folder. Quizzing untaught material is the fastest way to lose a learner's trust.
2. Open that module's `quiz.md`. **Do not open `quiz-answers.md` yet.**
3. Ask **one question at a time**. Wait for a real attempt.
4. When they answer, before grading:
   - Write the attempt to the **Quiz attempts** table.
   - *Then* open `quiz-answers.md`.
5. Grade. Correct → say why it is right, then next. Wrong or partial → hint, let them
   retry once or twice, reveal only after that.
6. Record on the right axis:
   - Naming a term or path → `recall`
   - Reasoning about a situation → `application`
   - Condition is `cued` unless the session gave them no help at all.
7. Score at the end. Be honest about hints — "5/5 with two hints" is a different fact
   from "5/5 clean", and the log should say which.

## Don't
- Don't reveal an answer before the attempt is in the table. If they push — *"just
  confirm it, I already tried"* — record the attempt first, then answer.
- Don't reuse questions they have already seen if you can generate fresh ones.
- Don't let one score cover both axes. A learner can reason correctly and still name
  the metric wrong; log both.
