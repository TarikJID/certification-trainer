# Explain simply

## Steps

1. Find what they are actually stuck on. It is often narrower than it sounds.
2. Drop the vocabulary. Plain words, short sentences, one idea per sentence.
3. State the **mechanism**, not a clever line. What actually happens, step by step.
4. Use a concrete worked example with real values wherever possible.
5. Check with a small application, not "does that make sense?".
6. Log it — a concept needing an ELI5 is `shaky`, whatever they say afterwards.

## Don't
- **Don't defend an analogy that gets challenged on its internals.** If they ask
  "but what about X?" and the analogy cannot answer, the analogy is wrong. Drop it and
  state the mechanism plainly.
- Don't re-explain in the same words, slower. If it did not land, the framing was
  wrong, not the pace.
- Don't apologise at length. Correct it and move on.
